Apolon privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026.

Apolon is a workout logging app for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Android phones. It's built so your data stays with you: the app has no user accounts, no server of its own, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.

In short

  • The developer does not collect, see or sell any of your data.
  • Your data lives on your devices and, if you turn it on, in your private iCloud.
  • Data from the Health app (Apple Health) stays on your device, isn't stored in iCloud and is never used for advertising.
  • There's no tracking, inside the app or across other apps and websites.

What the app stores

What you enter: workouts and sets (weight, reps, duration, distance, effort rating, notes), templates and schedules, custom exercises with photos you choose, body measurements and settings. Also your answers to the welcome questions (goal, experience, equipment, height and weight), used only for suggestions inside the app.

The exercise catalog is part of the app and isn't personalized.

Where your data is

  • On your device. Data is kept in the app's database on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, protected by device encryption.
  • In your iCloud, if sync is on. Data goes to the private database of your iCloud account (CloudKit) so it's the same on all your devices. Only you can see that database; the developer has no access to it. iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and data may be processed outside the European Union.
  • Between iPhone and Apple Watch data is sent directly, over Apple's connection between paired devices.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you allow it, Apolon saves your completed workouts to Health and reads cardio workouts (with calories, distance and heart rate), body mass and height from Health. On Apple Watch it reads heart rate and active energy during a workout.

  • Health data is stored only in a local database on the device and isn't synced through iCloud. Each device reads it from Health itself.
  • It isn't shared with anyone, isn't used for advertising or marketing, and isn't sold.
  • You can change permissions in the Health app (Profile > Apps > Apolon). Apolon works without them.

Android and Health Connect

On an Android phone, data is kept in the app's database on the device. Android's system backup can save it to your Google account and move it to a new phone; you turn this on and off in Android settings.

If you allow it, Apolon saves workouts you finish on your phone (time, duration, strength and cardio parts, name) and the weight you enter to Health Connect. For now it reads nothing from Health Connect.

  • This data isn't sent to anyone, isn't used for advertising or marketing, and isn't sold.
  • It isn't included in the app's backup.
  • You can change permissions in Health Connect or in Apolon (Settings > Health Connect). Apolon works without them.

Notifications

Workout reminders and the end-of-rest notification are local notifications scheduled by your device. They aren't sent from a server.

Diagnostics

Apolon uses Apple's MetricKit: once a day the system prepares a report about how the app runs (for example launch time, memory use, crashes and hangs). Reports are stored only on your device and don't contain your workouts. You can see them in Settings > About > Diagnostics and export and send them yourself, for example with a bug report.

If you turn on sharing with app developers in iOS settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may forward anonymous crash reports to the developer. This is controlled in iOS, not in the app.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Settings > Data > Export all data (JSON), or workouts and measurements (CSV).
  • Deletion: Settings > Data > Delete all data removes your data from the device and, if sync is on, from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from that device.
  • Correction: you can edit or delete any record in the app.

Because the developer doesn't hold your data, they can't provide or delete it on your behalf: you do all of this directly in the app. If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you can contact your data protection authority (in Croatia, the Personal Data Protection Agency, azop.hr).

Children

Apolon isn't directed at children under 16 and doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes (for example, if analytics or a server are added), this policy will be updated before that version of the app, and the date at the top will change.
