Apollo support

Apollo is a workout log for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Here are answers to common questions and how to report a bug or suggest something new.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account? No. Apollo has no account or sign-in. Your data is stored on your device and, if sync is on, in your iCloud.

How do I move my data to a new device? Sign in with the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud for Apollo (Settings > iCloud > iCloud sync). You can also export a JSON backup in Settings > Data and import it on the new device.

My workouts don't show up on another device. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is on and that sync is on in Apollo. The first sync can take a few minutes.

My watch doesn't show the workout from iPhone. Open Apollo on the watch while your iPhone is nearby. One device leads the workout and the other follows it.

How do I turn off Health access? In the Health app: profile > Apps > Apollo.

How do I delete all my data? Settings > Data > Delete all data. This deletes data on your device and in iCloud.

Bug reports and suggestions

Open a report at github.com/brunodobric/GymApp/issues. Include your device, iOS and app version (Settings > About) and the steps that lead to the problem. If the app crashed, you can export a report in Settings > About > Diagnostics and attach it.
