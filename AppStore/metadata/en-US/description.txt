Apolon is a gym workout log built so that logging never gets in the way of training: you log a set in a few taps, and what you did last time is right there in the same row.

TRAINING
• Previous results next to every set, with suggested weight and reps for the next one
• Rest timer starts on its own after a set, with +10 and −10 seconds
• Supersets, warm-up and working sets, RPE or RIR
• Exercises with weight, bodyweight, added weight, assistance or duration
• Plate calculator for your bar and the plates you have
• An interrupted workout (a call, a dead phone) picks up where it stopped

SCHEDULES
• Ready-made programs: Full Body, Upper / Lower and Push / Pull / Legs
• Your own schedules with cycles and rest days
• Reminders on fixed days or flexibly, when it suits you
• Change just the current cycle without touching the plan

EXERCISE CATALOG
• Over 180 exercises with images and instructions in English and Croatian
• Search and filters by muscle, equipment and movement type
• Your own exercises, with a photo

PROGRESS
• Personal records: weight, volume, reps, duration and distance
• Estimated 1RM and a progress chart for every exercise
• Volume per muscle, training frequency and a body heatmap
• Body measurements with a chart for each one

APPLE WATCH
• Sets and rest from your wrist, with heart rate and calories
• Complications for your next workout and the running rest timer
• Live Activity on iPhone while you train with your watch

IPAD
• Sidebar, multiple columns and larger charts for reviewing progress and building schedules

HEALTH AND ICLOUD
• Workouts are saved to Health and close your Activity rings
• Cardio, body mass and height from Health appear in your history and measurements
• Sync between iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch through your iCloud
• Export to CSV and JSON at any time

PRIVACY
Apolon has no account, ads, analytics or tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud, and Health data never leaves your device.
